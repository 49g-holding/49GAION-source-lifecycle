Each text now says what the complete definition says; no rule of this part is added or dropped. The wording replaced is listed below, verbatim.
2. **Cards.** Every card takes `v0.1.6`, generated by `tools/make_cards.py` from the blueprint, against the schemas of the Operating Protocol at the pin.
3. **The Protocol pin.** `PROTOCOL-PIN` and every `uses:` line of the checks move to the Operating Protocol `v2.2.0` (`afc91b6`), and the checks gain `links`: every relative link resolves.
4. **The Library pin.** `49GAION-protocol` `library/README.md` moves its pin of this blueprint to `v0.1.6` by its own pull request, once this one is merged.
